How I work

from idea to launch

Getting a new website or app built shouldn’t feel complicated. I’ll keep things clear, guide you through each stage, and make sure you always know what’s happening next.

01

Start with a chat

Tell me about your business, your idea and what you need the site or app to do. We’ll work out what matters most and agree on a clear plan before we get started.

02

Design

I’ll create the look, layout and key screens for you to review. You’ll see how it will work before anything is built, with plenty of room for feedback.

03

Build

Once the design is agreed, I build the website or app so it works smoothly across the devices your customers use. I keep you updated as it comes together.

04

Refine & launch

Before it goes live, I check the details, fix any final bits and make sure you’re happy with it. Then I’ll help get it launched and hand everything over clearly.

Good to know

A few common questions.

What do you design and build?

Custom websites, landing pages, app interfaces and the UX behind them.

How much does a website cost?

A focused one-page website is usually £350–£600, while a custom small-business website is typically £750–£1,500. More complex websites, apps and integrations are quoted individually. You’ll always receive a clear fixed quote before work begins.

Can I spread the cost?

Usually, yes. Larger website and app projects can normally be split into two or three staged payments. The payment schedule will be included in your custom quote so everything is clear before work starts.

How long does it take?

Most small websites take around two to four weeks once the content is ready. Larger websites and apps take longer, and I’ll give you a realistic schedule with your quote.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. I can review what is already working, improve the design and user experience, and rebuild the parts that are holding it back without losing what makes your business recognisable.

Do you provide hosting?

Yes. The optional Site Care plan is £29 per month and includes managed hosting, SSL, backups, monitoring and technical maintenance. You can also use your own provider if you prefer; any recurring costs are always agreed before launch.

Will I appear on Google?

Your website will be built so search engines can crawl and understand it, with essential technical SEO included. No one can guarantee a particular ranking, but I’ll give the site a strong foundation and show you the next steps for improving visibility.

Can I edit it myself?

Yes. If you need to update text, images or pages regularly, I can include a straightforward content management system and show you how to use it. We’ll agree what you need to edit before the build begins.

What happens after launch?

I’ll complete final checks, hand over the website and explain how everything works. You can manage it yourself, choose Site Care for the technical essentials, or use Care + Content for maintenance and regular updates. There is no requirement to take a monthly plan.

Can you do both the design and the build?

Yes — strategy, UX, visual design, the responsive build, launch and handover, so the whole thing feels coherent from the first click.

Who's a good fit?

Founders, small teams and growing businesses who want a clearer presence, a polished product, or a site that looks credible and brings in enquiries.

Do you only work with local businesses?

Not at all. I work locally across Blyth, Northumberland and North East England, as well as remotely with clients anywhere in the UK or worldwide.
