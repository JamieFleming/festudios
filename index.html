A little about me

Design and code
always made sense
together.

Jamie Fleming

Founder of FE Studios

I first found web design while studying computer science at university. I had always enjoyed both design and computing, and websites gave me a place to bring the two together. After years of continuing to practise in the background, I started FE Studios to properly return to the work I enjoy.

I’m also a dad of two and, when time allows, a runner, cyclist and enthusiastic explorer of the outdoors. Balancing work, family and the studio has taught me the value of clear communication, careful planning and making every hour count. My favourite part is taking an idea from a blank screen to a finished product that someone is proud to use. I especially enjoy working with people who care deeply about their business and see their website as an investment—not just another box to tick.
