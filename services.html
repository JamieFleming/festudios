Good to know

Common
questions.

If your question is not covered here, send it over. An initial conversation is free and does not commit you to starting a project.

How much does a website cost?+

A focused one-page website starts from £350, while a custom small-business website starts from £750. These are guides rather than fixed package prices: scope, complexity, timescale and budget all shape the final quote. More complex websites, apps and integrations are quoted individually.

How long does it take?+

Most small websites take around two to four weeks once the content is ready. Larger websites and apps take longer, and I provide a realistic schedule with the quote.

Can I spread the cost?+

Usually, yes. Larger website and app projects can normally be split into two or three staged payments, with the schedule agreed before work starts.

What is included in a website project?+

Every proposal defines exactly what is included. A typical website project covers planning, responsive design, development, an enquiry route, essential technical SEO, testing, launch support and a clear handover. Any optional hosting, copywriting, photography or third-party costs are identified separately.

What will you need from me?+

I usually need your business information, brand assets, page content, images and timely feedback. If those pieces are not ready, I can help you work out what is needed and include suitable support in the proposal.

Can you redesign my existing website?+

Yes. I can review what is working, improve the design and user experience, and rebuild what is holding it back without losing what makes the business recognisable.

Do you provide hosting?+

Yes. Site Care includes managed hosting, SSL, backups, monitoring and technical maintenance. You can also use your own provider if you prefer; recurring costs are agreed before launch.

Will I appear on Google?+

Your website will be built so search engines can crawl and understand it, with essential technical SEO included. No one can guarantee a ranking, but the site will have a strong foundation and clear next steps for improving visibility.

Can I edit the website myself?+

Yes. If you need regular changes, I can include a straightforward content management system and show you how to use it. We agree what needs to be editable before the build begins.

Do you only work with local businesses?+

No. I work locally across Blyth, Northumberland and North East England, and remotely with clients anywhere in the UK or worldwide.
